# Timeline of Talos-II

Tier key: **[Confirmed]** = in-game Nexus Files / official text. **[Community]** = labeled
consensus from lore communities (may shift with updates). **[Unknown]** = open mystery —
never assert, only use as a hook.

## At a glance

- **[Confirmed]** ~152 years ago, Terran pioneers from Terra reopen the Æthergate
  (also called the Cosmic Gate) and cross to Talos-II, second moon of the gas giant Talos.
- **[Confirmed]** Over the following century, stranded generations build outposts and towns
  across the moon, spreading along the Civilization Band. Rhodes Island and partners found
  Endfield Industries for colonization, industry, and defense.
- **[Confirmed]** The First Aggeloi War destroys the gate and cuts Talos-II off from Terra.
  The Northern Frontier is destroyed within 24 hours of the war breaking out.
- **[Confirmed]** In the present, the Endministrator awakens into a chaotic Talos-II.
  Under Supervisor Perlica, the Protocol Recycling Department is rebuilt and selects
  Valley No. 4 — the most barren region — as the proving ground for the AIC.
- **[Community]** Endfield sits ~152 years after Terra's story (Terran years ~1098–1102);
  the exact gap is not fixed and may be larger.

## Eras

### The Crossing (~152 years ago) [Confirmed]
Terran pioneers reopen the gate and set foot on a moon with nitrogen-rich air, oxygen,
greenhouse warmth, mountain ranges, river systems, and dense vegetation. In-world
astronomers consider that habitability suspiciously convenient given the moon's position —
and the gate's presence. Of Talos's many moons, only Talos-II is habitable.

### The Expansion (the century after) [Confirmed]
Generations of stranded Terrans and their descendants raise outposts, towns, and production
belts. Rhodes Island and partner organizations establish Endfield Industries as think tank,
forward prospector, and shield: colonization, industrial development, defense against
the Aggeloi. Its authority later rests on rebuilding civilization after isolation.

### The Severance — First Aggeloi War [Confirmed core, contested date]
The Æthergate is destroyed during the First Aggeloi War and the colony is left to itself.
The war is named for the enemy fought. **Date dispute [Community]:** some sources place the
destruction only ~5 years after landing; others roughly a century later. Treat any exact
year as unreliable in-universe — records from the war are fragmentary.

### The Rebuilding (decades before present) [Confirmed]
With the major powers resuming ruin exploration, the Protocol Recycling Department is
rebuilt under Perlica's leadership to prove Protocol transmission technology. Target:
Valley No. 4, chosen precisely because it is the most barren ground on Talos-II.

### The Awakening (present) [Confirmed frame, Community detail]
The Endministrator — Endfield's legendary leader, said to appear whenever crisis strikes —
awakens from long hibernation **[Community: reported as ten years]** with patchy memory,
and resumes command of the frontier effort.
