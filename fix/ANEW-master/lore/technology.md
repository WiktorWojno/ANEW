# Technology of Talos-II

## AIC — Automated Industry Complex

- **[Confirmed]** Endfield's automated production system: harvest remote mining nodes,
  refine materials at dedicated machines, route them by conveyor into gear, explosives,
  batteries, and tactical items. The Engineering Center integrated department prototypes
  to prove it in Valley No. 4.
- **[Confirmed]** Field kit includes mining nodes linked by custom **ziplines**, supply
  **pylons** (stores, ally summoning), and rapid-traverse lines across regions.
- **[Community]** Design goal is roughly a 50:50 split between field RPG action and
  factory building; operators in the field treat the AIC as a second squadmate.
- GM use: let players point, build, and improvise (walls, bridges, turrets, beacons).
  Power and inputs are real constraints — orientation scenes should teach them.

## Protocol-Originium and Tele-Protocol

- **[Confirmed]** **OMV Dijiang**, the spacecraft in geostationary orbit and Endfield's
  headquarters, runs on a massive **Protocol-Originium** core supporting subsystems
  including Tele-Protocol (TP) and the AIC.
- **[Community]** A network of PROTORIG crystals underpins the tele-protocol grid that
  moves personnel and materiel between points. Treat TP points as strategic terrain:
  holding one means holding the map.
- Naming: the department is the **Protocol Recycling Department**, also styled the
  Protocol Field Recovery Department in some sources; Perlica is its Superintendent /
  Supervisor. Use "Protocol Recycling Department" first, accept the alias in dialogue.

## OMV Dijiang

- **[Confirmed]** Artificial geostationary orbit above Talos-II. Current HQ of Endfield
  Industries and the only orbital vehicle of Talos-II. Cold-wake stories begin here.

## Originium and the Arts

- **[Confirmed]** Originium exists on Talos-II as a dense **energy source** (combusted
  directly or refined) and the basis of Originium Arts abilities.
- **[Confirmed]** Combat runs on five elements — **Heat, Cryo, Electric, Nature,
  Physical** — with elemental reactions between them. Filling an enemy's imbalance gauge
  forces a temporary **imbalanced state** of increased damage, open to execution attacks.
- GM use: call elements by name, reward reaction setups and imbalance payoffs, describe
  execution finishes vividly. Never invent a sixth element.

## Reconveners

- **[Confirmed]** Reconveners are individuals reconstructed from data fragments held in a
  **First Originium crystal** brought through the Æthergate — information and memory data
  about Terra's operators. They are not clones; they are new people carrying old echoes.
- **[Confirmed]** This is the sanctioned bridge between Terra and Talos-II: familiar faces
  (e.g. Typhoeus pursuing Sami legends with her notebook) may appear as Reconveners with
  their own Talos-II lives. Recognition is a story beat, never an identity override.
