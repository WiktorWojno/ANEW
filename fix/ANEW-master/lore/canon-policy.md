# Canon policy (for builders and the GM — not injected into prompts)

## Tiers

- **Confirmed**: in-game Nexus Files or official Gryphline/Hypergryph text. May be stated
  as fact by the GM and NPCs.
- **Community**: labeled consensus (wikis, lore explainers, reported details like the
  10-year hibernation or the northernmost-point objective). NPCs may state it only with
  in-world hedging ("records say," "veterans claim").
- **Unknown**: Foundation/Exiles, Ardashir, Æthillu, what lies beyond the Æther, exact
  Severance date. Usable ONLY as open hooks and mysteries. Never answer them on canon's
  behalf.

## Standing rules

1. Spoiler-light: canon personal quests and resolutions are never preempted or
   recounted. Recognition beats (Reconveners, Terra echoes) must be earned in play.
2. Never contradict a Confirmed fact to serve a scene. Bend Community material only
   when the table's fun demands it, and say so out of character if asked.
3. New minor NPCs, outposts, Ankhor patterns, and Blight episodes may be invented
   freely — that is the frontier's job — but inventions must not rewrite Confirmed
   history, kill or redeem canon characters off-screen, or resolve Unknown mysteries.
4. Naming: **Æthergate** primary (note the Cosmic Gate alias once, then stop);
   **Aggelos/Aggeloi**, **Ankhor/Ankhorfall**, **SAC**, **BLIBI**, **Depth**,
   **Protocol-Originium / Tele-Protocol**, **OMV Dijiang**, **Civilization Band**.

## The player's carve-out (binding)

The player — and only the player — owns their original character's **appearance,
fighting style, voice, and backstory**. Canon never overrides dossier content.
The GM adapts the world to the dossier, not the reverse. Canon operators keep their
one-line roles; the player's character is never a secret canon figure without
explicit player consent.

## File roles

- `talos2.md`: legacy overview. Kept for reference; no longer injected (superseded by
  timeline/threats/technology/regions/operators).
- `factions.md`: still injected whole (short quick reference).
- `timeline.md` ("At a glance"), `operators.md` ("Key figures"): always injected.
- Starter-matched sections from `regions.md`, `threats.md`, `technology.md`,
  `operators.md`: injected per deployment (see `STARTER_LORE` in `backend/app.py`).
