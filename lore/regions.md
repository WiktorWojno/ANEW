# Regions of Talos-II

## Valley IV

- **[Confirmed]** A frontier production region on the fringes of the Civilization Band,
  chosen by the United Workers' Syndicates as their ground — and by the Protocol
  Recycling Department as the AIC proving ground because it is the most barren area
  on Talos-II. Conveyors, pylons, welders, union crews.
- GM use: default OC arrival zone. AIC orientation, labor politics, Ankhorfall raids.

## OMV Dijiang

- **[Confirmed]** Orbital HQ (see technology). Cold-wake stories begin here: Perlica's
  briefing rooms, docking spars, the long view down at the storm-wracked moon.
- GM use: default Admin arrival zone. Briefings, department politics, deployment orders.

## Snowy Forest and Wuling

- **[Confirmed]** Wuling is a known region; the supernatural **Snowy Forest** carries
  time-loop rumors and is the investigation ground of the Typhoeus thread
  ("Dreamscape of Wind and Snow").
- GM use: default Canon-investigation zone. Loops, partitioned memories, Sami echoes.
  Hallucinations here may be Blight-adjacent — or grief. Keep both readings alive.

## Jinlong

- **[Confirmed]** A known region under heavy Talos-II General Chamber of Commerce
  influence: contracts, supply chains, armed messengers.
- GM use: trade stories, embargoes, deniable deals. Avywenna knows these roads.

## Northern Frontier

- **[Confirmed]** Destroyed within 24 hours when the First Aggeloi War broke out.
  Now wilderness, ruins, and Static Blight — and the direction the endgame points:
  **[Community]** the push toward the planet's northernmost point to preserve
  civilization, shielding the massive Originium Reactor on the way.
- GM use: late-story magnet, not an early drop zone. Mention it the way sailors mention
  the far side of a storm.

## Sami thread

- **[Confirmed]** On Talos-II, legends of Sami have mostly faded; a handful of Samifjod
  still remember them. Typhoeus visits them one by one, recording shattered memories
  in her notebook — and after reaching Endfield, the stories she pursues are no longer
  confined to forgotten legends.
- GM use: lore-reward thread for curious players. Recognition of Terra echoes is earned
  through legwork, never handed out.
