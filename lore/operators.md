# Dramatis personae (canon operators)

Rule for all entries: the GM may voice these characters and spend their reputations,
but never restat the player, never contradict the one-line canon role, and never resolve
a Reconverer's identity for them. Spoiler-light: no personal-quest resolutions.

## Key figures (always injected)

- **[Confirmed]** **Perlica** — Supervisor/Superintendent of Endfield Industries'
  Protocol Recycling Department. Every division reports through her. Crisp, tireless,
  protective of her people; briefs the Endministrator and processes new hires alike.
- **[Confirmed]** **The Endministrator** — legendary leader of Endfield Industries, the
  outfit's "trump card," said to appear whenever crisis strikes Talos-II or Endfield.
  Wakes from long hibernation with patchy memory. (Player-playable in Admin mode.)
- **[Confirmed]** **Chen Qianyu** — specialist operator of Endfield Industries and
  Perlica's personal security detail. Disciplined swordmaster.
- **[Confirmed]** **Wulfgard** — young mercenary on civilization's fringe, former member
  of a Landbreaker clan. Gruff veteran energy.
- **[Confirmed]** **Akekuri** — captain of Operation Team Z7 (with Antal, Catcher,
  Estella, Fluorite). Earnest field leader.

## Endfield Industries

- **[Confirmed]** Yvonne, Rossi, Tangtang — other Endfield members met in the field.
- **[Community]** Z7 reads as the player's first squad: Antal (striker), Catcher,
  Estella (cover fire), Fluorite. Keep them competent; let the player be the difference.

## Rhodes Island

- **[Confirmed]** **Angelina** — Rhodes operator dispatched to Endfield; gifted combatant
  wielding the rare anti-gravity aspect of Originium Arts.
- **[Confirmed]** Ardelia, Gilberta, Laevatain, Pogranichnik, Snowshine — Rhodes operators
  with Talos-II postings. Medical-humanitarian roots, veteran calm.

## Hongshan Academy of Sciences

- **[Confirmed]** Da Pan, Lifeng, Mi Fu (Zhuang Fangyi named in Academy records) — field
  scholars. Researchers first: they sample the Blight so others don't have to.
