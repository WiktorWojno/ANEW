# Talos-II Canon Bible (spoiler-light, for GM grounding)

## World
Talos-II is a habitable moon of gas giant Talos. Nitrogen-rich air, mountains, river systems, resilient biosphere. Terran pioneers came through the Aethergate 152 years ago. Gate collapsed. Stranded 100+ years. Descendants built outposts and towns along the Civilization Band.

Sky reminders: alien sun + looming gas giant. Locals say the habitability is no coincidence — Aethergate link suspected.

## Endfield Industries
Founded jointly by Rhodes Island + partners as think-tank / forward prospector. Now the frontier leader.
- Leader: Endministrator (Endmin) — alive through Talos-II history, long stasis, memory loss on wake.
- Supervisor / liaison: Perlica — all divisions report through her.
- Operation Team Z7: Captain Akekuri, Antal, Catcher, Estella, Fluorite.
- Other members: Chen Qianyu, Wulfgard, Yvonne, Rossi, Tangtang.
- HQ: OMV Dijiang (only orbital vehicle of Talos-II).
- Tech: AIC factory (Automated Industry Complex), Protocol-Originium teleport, controlled Originium distribution.

Creed: assist comrades, gather people, rekindle alliances, never give up.

## Threats
Aggeloi (hostile entities), Blight / Erosion zones, Originium anomalies, storms, broken terrain.

## Other factions
- Rhodes Island: Ardelia, Gilberta, Laevatain, Pogranichnik, Snowshine.
- Hongshan Academy of Sciences: Da Pan, Lifeng, Mi Fu, Zhuang Fangyi.
- United Workers Syndicate of Talos-II: Alesh + Valley IV production crews.
- Talos-II General Chamber of Commerce: Avywenna.
- Hannabit Circuit: Arclight. Order of Steel Oath: Ember. Cabal of Tranquility: Xaihi. Seš'qa: Camille, Last Rite.

## Regions (starting)
- Valley IV: frontier production belt, UWST crews, AIC tutorial zone.
- Wuling / Snowy Forest: supernatural forest, time-loop rumors, Typhoeus / Purrchena thread.
- Jinlong: trade / Chamber of Commerce influence.
- OMV Dijiang: orbital HQ, cold-wake intro.

## Terms to use correctly
Endministrator, Supervisor Perlica, AIC, Protocol, Originium, Aggeloi, Blight/Erosion, Aethergate, Civilization Band, OMV Dijiang.
