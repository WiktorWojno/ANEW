# Threats of Talos-II

## Aggeloi

- **[Confirmed]** The Aggelos (plural Aggeloi) is any hostile animated entity of natural
  matter plus a halo structure, created by an **Ankhor** through catalytic construction.
  Humanity's primary threat across its whole expansion on Talos-II.
- **[Confirmed]** Four verified behavior modes: (1) construction, (2) deconstruction,
  (3) mimicry, (4) attacking humans and human-built objects. Because Ankhor-made formation
  resembles industrial production, whether Aggeloi count as living beings is genuinely
  uncertain in-universe.
- **[Confirmed]** Each Ankhor produces a limited range of **construct patterns** (appearance,
  movement, attack), with occasional off-pattern variants. An Ankhor's property is defined
  by the patterns it can create. Since the First Aggeloi War, new patterns feed the
  **Standard Aggeloid Classification (SAC)** system — use SAC terms when operators assess
  weaknesses and tactics.
- **[Confirmed]** Most current threats are raids from unknown sources or random
  **Ankhorfalls**. The Aggelos remains the most dangerous challenge to civilization
  on Talos-II.
- Voice rule: Aggeloi do not speak, bargain, or monologue. They build, unbuild, mimic,
  and attack.

## Blight

- **[Confirmed]** The Blight is the general term for hazardous subterranean, surface,
  atmospheric, and orbital phenomena of Talos-II. Two categories: **Active Blight** and
  **Static Blight** (the residue left when an Active episode subsides).
- **[Confirmed]** Classic Active cases: **Blightfloods** on the surface, **Blight Tides**
  in water — often with strange fluorescent color displays.
- **[Confirmed]** Active Blight inflicts dissociative and/or inversive destruction at
  variable rates: it can deprive matter and/or time from the space it manifests in —
  rapid and absolute, or slow and slight.
- **[Confirmed]** Active episodes decay into Static Blight (like fluid asphalt over the
  wreckage) after anywhere from 217 milliseconds to over 149 years on record.
- **[Confirmed]** Effects on minds: survivors report hallucinations, sudden stress, and
  other conditions even without brain injury. Almost all true Blight-induced brain injury
  (BLIBI) cases do not survive.
- **[Confirmed]** A century of data links Active Blight to positive **Depth readings**,
  incidence following a bell curve over Depth 0–1. Operators treat rising Depth the way
  sailors treat a falling barometer.
- Related **[Confirmed]**: Talos-II suffers Terra-like **Catastrophes**, most notoriously
  the environment-warping supernatural disaster called **Corruption**.

## Human threats

- **[Confirmed]** **Landbreakers**: armed raiders threatening outposts and convoys. Human,
  negotiable, betrayable — unlike Aggeloi. Wulfgard is a former member of a Landbreaker
  clan living on civilization's fringe.
- **[Community]** Corporate and union rivalries turn violent at the margins: claim-jumpers,
  embargo runners, and deniable sabotage crews. Keep these small and deniable — the major
  powers fight with contracts first.
